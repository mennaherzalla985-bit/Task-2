1. Introduction

web platforms are an important part of every day life. People use websites to study,shop, book services, ask questions, and communicate with organizations

Achatbot is a software programme that communicates with people through text or voice. It can answer questions, provide information, and help users complete tasks with out speaking to a human employee.

chatbots are now used on many websites and applications because they are fact, available at all times, and can help many users at the same time.

2. main types of chatbots

there are two main types of chatbots used in web platforms:

type How it works Example
Rule _ Based chatbot works with fixed rules and prepared answers.It follows a specific path. FAQ chatbot that answers common questions
AI_ powered chatbot uses Artificial Intelligence and natural language processing to understand the user and learn from conversation. virtual assistant that understands different ways of asking

3. common uses of chatbots

  • online shopping:Helps customers find products, track orders, and answer questions about delivery.
  • Education: Helps students find lessons, answer questions and remind them of assignments.
  • travel and services :Helps users book tickets, check schedules and get information about services .
  • customer support: Answers questions 24 hours aday without waiting.

6. Recommendations for Good Design

A good chatbot should be clear and easy to use.

4. conclusion

chatbots have become an important part of web platform. they help users quickly,reduse waiting time, and improve the quality of online services.in the future,chatbots will become smarter and more helpful with the development of Artificial Intelligence.
