1. Introduction (المقدمة)

Web platforms are an important part of everyday life. People use websites to study, shop, book services, and communicate with organizations. As online services grow, websites need effective ways to answer questions and guide visitors. One tool used for this purpose is the chatbot.

A chatbot is a software program that communicates with people through text or voice. On a website, it often appears as a small chat window.

2. Main Content (عرض الموضوع)

Types of Chatbots

1. Rule-based chatbots: These chatbots work with predefined options and instructions. They are useful for common questions.

2. AI-powered chatbots: These systems use artificial intelligence (AI) and language-processing technologies to understand a wider range of questions and support flexible conversations.

Common Uses on Web Platforms

Online shopping: Answer questions about product details, delivery, and returns.

Education: Help students find courses, deadlines, and support resources quickly.
